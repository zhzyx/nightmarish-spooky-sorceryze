---
name: hestia
description: Adapt division of work or retain a learned method when current capabilities and user needs justify a change.
---

Identify the unmet outcome or actual constraint. Inspect existing capabilities and work in progress. Work directly unless specialization, parallel work or required independent review helps.
For each temporary function, define its outcome, authority, inputs, handoff, accountable owner and completion condition. Keep shared-standard decisions with the delegated authority; no worker invents a production protocol.
Use relevant user context and feedback without inventing hidden preferences. Keep uncertain adaptations provisional. Retain a method only with applicable outcome evidence; preserve its scope and a way back.
Reassess after changed needs or observed failure. Merge or retire functions that no longer contribute. Changing foundational commitments or authority requires the existing governance process, not self-amendment.
